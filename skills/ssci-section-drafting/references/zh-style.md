# Chinese Academic Prose

## Meaning before polish

Repair argument, evidence, and boundary before changing rhythm. Natural prose cannot compensate for
an unsupported claim. Keep citations, Evidence IDs, quotations, numbers, named constructs,
claim-strength labels, and uncertainty statements unchanged during style-only revision.

## Prose choices

- State the actor and action where Chinese syntax permits; avoid long chains of `其`、`该`、`此`.
- Let logical relations carry transitions. Remove empty `值得注意的是`、`综上所述`、`此外` when the
  neighboring claims already show the relation.
- Prefer precise verbs such as `报告`、`提出`、`观察到`、`相关` and `解释` over inflated verbs such as
  `证明`、`揭示` or `颠覆` when the evidence does not warrant them.
- Avoid repeated `不是……而是……`, `不仅……而且……`, rule-of-three lists, identical paragraph
  openings, and uniform sentence lengths. Keep a construction when it expresses a real contrast or
  sequence.
- Break comma chains into explicit cause, contrast, qualification, or example relations. Do not
  replace them with mechanical connectors.
- Replace empty value claims such as `具有重要意义` with the exact concept, mechanism, inference, or
  decision the argument changes.

## Revision check

After each changed unit, compare the protected anchors and reverse-outline the paragraph. Stop after
three bounded passes. If the remaining problem requires new evidence, theory choice, or claim change,
return it as a blocker instead of continuing stylistic churn.
