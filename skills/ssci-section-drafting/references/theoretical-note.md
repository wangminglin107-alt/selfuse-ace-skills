# Chinese Theoretical Research Note

Use this reference only for a theoretical note or theory-centered literature argument.

## Required shape

1. Open with the bounded phenomenon and research puzzle; do not use a generic historical preface.
2. Reconstruct the scholarly tension by mechanism or disagreement, not one-author-per-sentence.
3. State the selected theoretical lens exactly as approved upstream and explain what it clarifies.
4. Develop the argument through claims whose evidence and counterevidence are both visible.
5. Close with the note's contribution, scope conditions, unresolved evidence, and next empirical test.

For the GSMA tracer project, target 4,000–6,000 Chinese characters excluding references and
appendices. This band is project-specific, not a universal quota.

## Evidence discipline

- Every durable literature claim must point to one or more Evidence IDs whose source passage and
  locator were inspected.
- Use metadata-only or abstract-only sources to map a conversation, never to carry a durable claim.
- A Chinese paraphrase must preserve the source's actor, modality, causal status, population, and
  boundary. Keep the exact original-language passage in the trace artifact.
- Synthesize contradictions by comparing design, platform, period, unit, and inference; do not erase
  them to create a smooth narrative.

## Paragraph test

Each paragraph has one job, one leading claim or analytic move, enough evidence to warrant it, and
an explicit relation to the section thesis. Vary paragraph length when the argument requires it;
do not impose a repeated claim-explanation-summary shell.
