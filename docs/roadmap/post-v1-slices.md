# Post-V1 vertical slices

Each slice must receive a design amendment and implementation plan. It reuses contract `1.0`,
state, checkpoints, providers, gates, and `SOURCE_MANIFEST.yaml` unless a migration is approved.
The order below is the V1 freeze priority, not authorization to implement every slice at once.

## 1. Evidence synthesis and theory architecture

- Decisions: synthesis unit, contradiction model, theory-selection criteria, construct ontology,
  and boundary between source findings and author inference.
- Upstream sources to inspect: AI Research Writing synthesis/citation mechanisms, AcademicSkills
  theory workflows, and the existing local SSCI argument-architecture Skills.
- Providers: `local-manual` first; optional Zotero/CNKI/international discovery adapters only after
  privacy and provenance contracts are approved.
- Gates: content-verification sufficiency, claim-to-source coverage, contradiction preservation,
  construct consistency, and theory/evidence fit.
- Acceptance artifacts: synthesis matrix, contradiction ledger, theory candidate table,
  construct map, and evidence-bounded theory rationale.

## 2. Research design and method audit

- Decisions: supported methodological families, design preregistration boundary, causal-language
  policy, sampling representation, and discipline-specific specializations.
- Upstream sources to inspect: AcademicSkills method modules, selected local SSCI methods guidance,
  and reusable validation patterns from upstream research-writing projects.
- Providers: local datasets/protocols by default; optional survey, repository, or registry adapters
  require explicit declarations.
- Gates: question-design fit, construct-operationalization traceability, sampling limits, ethics and
  privacy, identification claims, and reproducibility completeness.
- Acceptance artifacts: design brief, variable/construct map, sampling plan, analysis plan, ethics
  checklist, and method-audit report.

## 3. Evidence interpretation and numeric verification

- Decisions: supported analysis engines, tolerance/rounding rules, missing-data policy, effect-size
  conventions, and boundary between computation and interpretation.
- Upstream sources to inspect: analysis/verification utilities from approved academic repositories
  and local quantitative/qualitative method Skills.
- Providers: local R/Python/statistical outputs; no remote compute by default.
- Gates: source-data hash, result reproducibility, table/text agreement, uncertainty propagation,
  robustness coverage, and prohibited causal extrapolation.
- Acceptance artifacts: analysis manifest, verified result tables, numeric-check ledger,
  robustness matrix, and bounded interpretation brief.

## 4. Paper architecture, section drafting, and bilingual alignment

- Decisions: target article genres, journal constraints, section ownership, citation insertion
  boundary, bilingual terminology authority, and acceptable human-review stops.
- Upstream sources to inspect: AI Research Writing, Spark-to-Paper, AcademicSkills, and the seven
  preserved local SSCI writing/revision Skills.
- Providers: verified local evidence graph only; translation/model providers require declared data
  handling.
- Gates: claim-evidence coverage, argument continuity, section contract, citation presence,
  terminology alignment, and no unsupported strengthening during translation.
- Acceptance artifacts: paper architecture, claim outline, section drafts, bilingual termbase,
  alignment report, and manuscript checkpoint set.

## 5. Figure intelligence

- Decisions: supported figure types, chart-selection rules, editable output formats, visual style,
  accessibility requirements, and numeric source of truth.
- Upstream sources to inspect: SciPilot Figure source files and licensed social-science
  visualization guidance.
- Providers: local plotting engines; image generation remains optional and cannot replace data
  figures.
- Gates: data-to-mark traceability, label/unit correctness, visual non-deception, accessibility,
  caption/evidence agreement, and reproducible export.
- Acceptance artifacts: figure specification, source-data mapping, editable figure, rendered
  preview, caption, and visual-audit report.

## 6. Citation verification, reviewer simulation, and revision

- Decisions: bibliographic authority, full-text verification level, reviewer personas, revision
  acceptance rules, and how contested recommendations are recorded.
- Upstream sources to inspect: AI Research Writing citation lock, AcademicSkills review modules,
  and the preserved local SSCI peer-review/revision Skills.
- Providers: Zotero/local PDFs first; external bibliographic providers only through declared,
  auditable adapters.
- Gates: citation identity, passage support, retraction/correction status, reviewer-evidence link,
  revision completeness, and regression of previously passed claims.
- Acceptance artifacts: citation audit, anonymous review set, issue ledger, response matrix,
  revised manuscript, and before/after gate report.

## 7. Publication packaging

- Decisions: supported journals/templates, anonymization rules, reporting checklists, disclosure
  policy, supplementary-material scope, and submission-system boundary.
- Upstream sources to inspect: licensed packaging/checklist components from approved repositories
  and journal-owned primary instructions supplied for the target submission.
- Providers: local filesystem only by default; submission portals remain human-controlled.
- Gates: journal-format conformance, anonymity, artifact completeness, cross-reference integrity,
  disclosure presence, and final provenance freeze.
- Acceptance artifacts: submission manuscript, title page, highlights/abstract as required,
  supplements, checklist bundle, cover-letter draft, and publication-readiness report.
