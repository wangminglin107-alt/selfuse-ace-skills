# SSCI Argument Architecture

## Boundary

Build manuscript structure from registered upstream artifacts. This capability does not select
theory, search literature, verify citations, or write manuscript prose.

## Contract

Capability: `ssci-argument-architecture`.

Consume the approved `theory_decision_packet`, `theory_rationale`, `synthesis_matrix`,
`contradiction_ledger`, `citation_support_audit`, and `evidence_rows`, plus the research brief and
novelty audit when available. Produce `paper_argument_map`, `section_outline`,
`claim_evidence_plan`, and `terminology_ledger`.

## Method

1. State one bounded thesis that answers the research question without exceeding the selected
   theory or verified evidence.
2. Map the paper's moves: problem, scholarly tension, theoretical response, supporting claims,
   counterevidence, boundary, and contribution.
3. Give each section and paragraph one argumentative job. Order by dependency rather than a fixed
   IMRAD template.
4. Link every durable claim to Evidence IDs and citation-support status. Downgrade, defer, or block
   unsupported claims; never fill gaps from model memory.
5. Fix preferred Chinese and English terms, definitions, levels of analysis, and prohibited drift.

The architecture must keep material contradictions visible. `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED` is an output
condition, not permission to invent a decision.
