# Contradictions and theory decisions

Evidence synthesis preserves opposition at the claim level. A group containing both supporting and
contradicting rows requires a contradiction-ledger entry. A material contradiction cannot remain
open at a completion boundary; it must be resolved, accepted as a stated limitation, or stop the
workflow. Non-material open tensions still require an explicit boundary note.

`theory-architecture` maps verified evidence to constructs, mechanisms, levels of analysis,
assumptions, and limitations. Cross-level relations require a rationale. When evidence does not
establish a defensible mechanism, `descriptive` is a valid recommendation; the system must not force
a named theory.

The final decision remains human-owned. `authorization_state: proposed` means the system has prepared
a reviewable packet but no theory has been adopted. `authorization_state: selected` is valid only
with a recorded `user_decision_id`. The `literature-to-theory` workflow stops at this review boundary
even in autonomous mode.
